Notes and definitions

Profit / loss = current value minus cost. Cost comes from your buying-price sheet; bonus and demerger shares (GSPL, Reliance, part of LIC) have zero cost, so their whole value counts as profit.

Only the main CDSL account is covered. IIFL Capital (7,500 shares, cost ₹25.1 L) is in your buying sheet but not in the 31 Oct statement, so it is left out. Visesh Infotecnics uses a buy price of 1.41 (value divided by quantity), not the 1.14 in your sheet.

Prices come from the Maa Kali Live Prices Google Sheet (Google Finance, NSE) and can be delayed by a few minutes. Shares with no live quote use their last saved price.
